✦ Quick Answers

Common questions

How quickly are vouchers delivered? ▼
Instantly! As soon as your payment is confirmed by PayFast, the voucher code and QR are sent directly to the recipient's WhatsApp. The whole process takes under 30 seconds.
What if my voucher hasn't arrived? ▼
Check that the WhatsApp number was entered correctly. If it's been more than 5 minutes after payment confirmation, WhatsApp us at +27 81 000 0000 with your order reference and we'll resend immediately.
Can I get a refund on a voucher? ▼
Unredeemed vouchers can be refunded within 48 hours of purchase. Once a voucher is redeemed, refunds are handled by the partner business directly. Contact us and we'll facilitate the process.
How do I apply to list my business? ▼
Visit our Partner Programme page and fill in the application form. We review all applications within 24 hours and contact you via WhatsApp.
Do you offer corporate accounts? ▼
Yes! We have dedicated corporate plans for teams of 10 to 10,000+. Visit our Corporate Gifting page or email corporate@giftsa.co.za.
Are payments secure? ▼
All payments are processed through PayFast with 256-bit SSL encryption. We never store card details. PayFast is a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment gateway.
