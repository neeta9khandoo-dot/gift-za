1. Overview

In plain English: We collect only what we need to run GiftSA. We never sell your data. We keep it secure and give you control over it.

AfriVoucher (Pty) Ltd ("GiftSA", "we", "us", or "our") operates the AfriVoucher platform at giftsa.co.za. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal information when you use our platform, whether as a customer, gift recipient, or partner business.

This policy is governed by the Protection of Personal Information Act, 4 of 2013 (POPIA), which is South Africa's primary data protection legislation. By using AfriVoucher you agree to this policy.

Our Information Officer responsible for compliance is the Chief Executive Officer, contactable at privacy@giftsa.co.za.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide directly

  • Name and email address when purchasing a voucher or creating a partner account
  • WhatsApp number of the voucher recipient
  • Personal gift messages you write during checkout
  • Business details (name, category, services) when applying as a partner
  • Messages sent to us via our contact form or email
  • Newsletter subscription email address

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • IP address and approximate location (country/city level)
  • Browser type, device type, and operating system
  • Pages viewed and time spent on each page
  • Referral source (e.g. Google, social media, direct)
  • Voucher codes generated, redeemed, and their timestamps

2.3 Payment information

We do not collect or store credit card numbers, CVV codes, or banking details. All payment processing is handled exclusively by PayFast (Pty) Ltd. We receive only a transaction confirmation and reference number.

4. Data Sharing

We share your personal data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Partner businesses: We share the recipient's name and voucher code with the relevant partner business when a voucher is purchased from them. We do not share email addresses or phone numbers with partners.
  • PayFast: Transaction details are shared with PayFast to process your payment. See PayFast's privacy policy at payfast.io.
  • Firebase / Google: Our platform database is hosted on Google Firebase. Data is stored in Google's servers under standard contractual clauses.
  • Twilio: WhatsApp voucher delivery is powered by Twilio. The recipient's phone number and voucher content are transmitted to Twilio's servers for delivery.
  • Legal requirement: We may disclose personal information if required by South African law, court order, or government authority.
We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for their marketing purposes.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purpose it was collected, or as required by law:

  • Voucher transaction records: 5 years (for tax and legal compliance)
  • Partner account data: Duration of the partnership + 3 years
  • Contact form messages: 2 years
  • Newsletter subscriber data: Until you unsubscribe
  • Website analytics data: 13 months (rolling)

After the relevant retention period, your data is securely deleted or anonymised.

6. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal data:

  • All data in transit is encrypted with TLS 1.3 (HTTPS)
  • Firebase database access is protected by strict security rules
  • Cloudflare R2 image storage with private access controls
  • Partner accounts protected by Firebase Authentication with email verification
  • Regular security reviews and dependency updates

No system is 100% secure. In the unlikely event of a data breach that poses a risk to your rights, we will notify you and the Information Regulator within 72 hours as required by POPIA.

7. Your Rights Under POPIA

As a South African data subject, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access: Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Right to correction: Request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • Right to deletion: Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Right to object: Object to the processing of your data for direct marketing at any time
  • Right to withdraw consent: Withdraw consent for newsletter communications at any time
  • Right to complain: Lodge a complaint with South Africa's Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@giftsa.co.za. We will respond within 30 days as required by POPIA.

8. Cookies

AfriVoucher uses minimal cookies to make the platform work. We do not use advertising cookies or sell cookie data.

  • Essential cookies: Required for the platform to function (session management, security). These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies: We use anonymised analytics to understand how people use GiftSA. No personal identifiers are tracked. You can opt out by enabling "Do Not Track" in your browser.

We do not use Facebook Pixel, Google Ads, or any retargeting cookies.

9. Children's Privacy

AfriVoucher is not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact privacy@giftsa.co.za and we will delete it promptly.

10. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or South African law. We will notify you of any material changes by:

  • Posting the updated policy on this page with the "Last updated" date
  • Sending an email notification to registered partner accounts
  • Displaying a notice on the AfriVoucher platform for 30 days

Continued use of AfriVoucher after a policy update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
