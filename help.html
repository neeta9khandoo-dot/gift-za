Buying Vouchers

❓
How do I buy a gift voucher on GiftSA?
Browse experiences on our store, click on any voucher you like, then click "Buy This Voucher." Fill in the recipient's name and WhatsApp number, add a personal message, and complete payment through PayFast. The voucher is sent instantly to the recipient's WhatsApp.
▼
❓
Can I buy a voucher for myself?
Absolutely! Just enter your own name and WhatsApp number as the recipient. You can also buy a voucher and keep it to redeem later — the code remains valid for the full validity period shown on the voucher.
▼
❓
How long are vouchers valid for?
Validity varies per experience — it's clearly shown on each voucher card (e.g. "Valid 12 months"). The expiry date is also included in the WhatsApp message the recipient receives. You can always contact the partner business to discuss extending validity in exceptional circumstances.
▼
❓
Can I add a personal message to the voucher?
Yes! During checkout you'll see a "Personal Note" field. Whatever you write there will be included in the WhatsApp message to the recipient. Keep it heartfelt — it makes the gift so much more special.
▼
❓
Is there a minimum or maximum purchase amount?
There's no minimum beyond the individual voucher price — the cheapest voucher currently listed is R480. There's no maximum. For bulk corporate purchases of 10+ vouchers, contact our corporate team for volume pricing.
▼
❓
Can I schedule delivery for a specific date?
Scheduled delivery is on our roadmap! Currently vouchers are sent immediately on payment. If you want to time a surprise, we suggest purchasing close to the date. Alternatively, contact us and we can manually hold delivery for you.
▼

Redeeming Vouchers

❓
How do I redeem my GiftSA voucher?
Contact the partner business directly (their details are in your WhatsApp voucher) to book a date. When you arrive, show the QR code or tell them your voucher code. The partner will scan or enter it in their GiftSA dashboard to mark it as redeemed. Easy as that.
▼
❓
My voucher code says "already redeemed" — what do I do?
Contact us immediately at hello@giftsa.co.za or WhatsApp +27 81 000 0000. We have a full audit trail for every voucher and can investigate instantly. If the code was redeemed in error, we will issue a replacement at no charge.
▼
❓
Do I need internet access to show my voucher?
No — once your voucher is in your WhatsApp, you can screenshot the QR code and show it offline. We recommend screenshotting your voucher as a backup when you receive it.
▼
❓
Can someone else redeem my voucher on my behalf?
Yes. Vouchers are transferable — whoever presents the valid code or QR to the partner business can redeem it. If you received a voucher as a gift but want to transfer it to someone else, just forward the WhatsApp message to them.
▼
📱

WhatsApp Delivery

❓
My voucher hasn't arrived on WhatsApp — what now?
First, double-check that you entered the correct WhatsApp number at checkout. The number must be active on WhatsApp — not just a regular phone number. If the number was correct and it's been more than 5 minutes since payment, WhatsApp us at +27 81 000 0000 and we'll resend it immediately.
▼
❓
The recipient's number wasn't on WhatsApp — can I change it?
Yes! Email us at hello@giftsa.co.za with your order reference and the correct WhatsApp number. We can resend to the right number within minutes. This is completely free of charge.
▼
❓
Can I get the voucher sent by email instead?
We are building email delivery — it's coming soon. For now, if WhatsApp isn't an option, contact us and we can generate a PDF voucher and email it to you manually.
▼

Payments & Billing

❓
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major South African payment methods through PayFast: Visa and Mastercard credit/debit cards, Instant EFT (all major banks), SnapScan, Zapper, and Mobicred. We do not currently accept cash or crypto.
▼
❓
Is my payment information secure?
Yes. All payments are processed by PayFast, a PCI DSS Level 1 certified payment gateway. GiftSA never sees or stores your card details. All transactions are encrypted with 256-bit SSL.
▼
❓
Can I get a tax invoice?
Yes — a VAT invoice is automatically emailed to you at the email address you provided during checkout. If you need it re-sent, email hello@giftsa.co.za with your order reference.
▼

Refunds & Changes

❓
Can I get a refund on my voucher?
Unredeemed vouchers can be refunded within 48 hours of purchase. Email hello@giftsa.co.za with your voucher code and the reason for the refund. Refunds are processed within 3–5 business days back to your original payment method. Redeemed vouchers cannot be refunded.
▼
❓
What if the partner business is closed or won't honour the voucher?
This is extremely rare as all our partners are vetted. If this happens, contact us immediately. We take partner accountability seriously and will either arrange the experience with an alternative partner or issue a full refund. We have never left a customer without a resolution.
▼
❓
Can I extend a voucher that's about to expire?
Voucher extensions are at the discretion of the partner business. Contact them directly to discuss — most partners are happy to extend expiry by up to 3 months in genuine circumstances. GiftSA cannot extend vouchers directly, but we can facilitate the conversation with the partner.
▼

Partner Accounts

❓
How do I register as a GiftSA partner?
Visit our Partner Programme page and submit an application. We review all applications within 24 hours. Once approved, you'll receive login credentials and can start creating vouchers immediately. The first 10 vouchers are pre-loaded for you.
▼
❓
How and when do partners get paid?
Partner payouts are processed every Friday via EFT. You'll receive 80%+ of each sale (the exact split depends on your agreement). Payouts include a breakdown of each voucher sold and redeemed during the week. Funds typically arrive in your account by Monday.
▼
❓
How do I add a new voucher to the store?
Log in to your Admin dashboard and click "Add New Voucher." Fill in the voucher name, category, price, description, what's included, and optionally upload a photo. Click "Create & Save Voucher" and it will appear on the store immediately. You can create as many vouchers as you like.
▼
❓
How do I validate a customer's voucher at my business?
Log in to your GiftSA partner account, go to the Redeem page, and enter the customer's voucher code (or scan their QR with your phone camera). The system will confirm if the voucher is valid and mark it as redeemed. This works on any smartphone or computer with internet access.
▼
