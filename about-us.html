✦ How We Started

Born out of a gifting problem

It started with a birthday. Our founder Neeta wanted to give her sister something truly memorable — not a voucher for a shop, not a gift card that gathers dust — but a real experience. A spa day. A sunset balloon ride. A cooking class. Something she'd actually talk about for months.

But finding, booking and sending that gift was harder than it should have been. The experience economy in South Africa was fragmented — great businesses, no easy way to reach them as a gift.

So we built GiftSA. A marketplace where every South African experience — from Magaliesberg adventures to Cape Winelands tastings — is just a few clicks away, delivered instantly to anyone in the country via WhatsApp.

Today we work with over 50 verified South African businesses and have helped thousands of people give gifts that actually matter.

✦ Our Journey

From idea to marketplace

Early 2024
The Idea
Frustrated by fragmented gifting options, Neeta sketches the first version of GiftSA on a napkin after her sister's birthday.
Mid 2024
First 10 Partners
We sign our first ten Pretoria-based businesses — spas, adventure operators, and a wine estate. The platform goes live in beta.
Late 2024
WhatsApp Delivery
We launch instant WhatsApp voucher delivery — turning what was a 24-hour process into something that happens in seconds.
2025
National Expansion
We expand to Cape Town, Durban and the Winelands. Over 2,800 customers served. 50+ verified partners. The journey is just beginning.
