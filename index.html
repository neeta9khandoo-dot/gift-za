✦ How We Started

A gift idea that became a marketplace.

It started in 2024 when our founder tried to gift a friend a spa day in Pretoria. Three hours of Googling, two dead phone numbers, and a voucher that never arrived later — the idea for GiftSA was born.

We built the platform we wished existed: instant WhatsApp delivery, vetted partners, and a seamless experience from purchase to arrival.
