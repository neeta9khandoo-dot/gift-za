Common questions

When do I get paid?

We do weekly EFT payouts every Friday for all vouchers sold that week. You'll receive an itemised statement showing each voucher sold, our fee, and your net amount.

What if a customer wants a refund?

Vouchers are non-refundable once issued (this protects you). Customers can however reschedule their bookings directly with you, subject to availability.

How do I know when a voucher is redeemed?

You'll receive a WhatsApp notification every time one of your vouchers is purchased. When the customer arrives, you scan or enter the code on our Redeem page to validate it instantly.

Can I set my own prices?

Absolutely. You tell us what price you want to list at and we'll set it accordingly. You can also update prices at any time — just message us.

Do I need a website or Takealot store?

No. VoucherHub ZA is your storefront. Customers find and buy your voucher directly on our platform, and the payment and delivery are handled automatically.

What happens to vouchers that are never redeemed?

Unredeemed vouchers expire after the validity period. The revenue stays shared as agreed — you keep your portion regardless of whether the customer books.
